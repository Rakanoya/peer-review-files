Brief Biography

John Stanley CooperJr. was born on June 1st, 1996, in Kingston-upon-Thames, England. Born to his mother, a photographer, and his father, a stand-up comedian and author. John is the oldest of four boys, he grew up in a tight-knit household with his brothers Josh, Harry, and Thomas.

At the age of 9 Josh began attending hip-hip dance classes at the Fancy Feet Dance studio, the owner, Nancy Page, Helped choreograph the hit movie Bobby Elliot. After John's class performed at the 2006 Richmond Dance Festival, Page was convinced to send him to audition for Bobby Elliot the Musical. John failed to impress most of the talent evaluators, but this marked the start of his love for performing.

Following a few years of ballet lessons, Cooper debuted with the North End production in June 2008, until his final performance in May 2010. Throughout his childhood, Cooper attended Wimbledon College before finishing his schooling at the BRIT School for Performing Arts and Technology.

In recent years Cooper has achieved international recognition playing Beter Barker/Arachnoman in multiple Carvel Cinematic Universe superhero films. Additionally John has had tons of voice acting experiences, as well as other big-budget films such as Charted, and The journey. John is engaged to married to his Arachnoman co-star Sendaya.

Career Goals

John aims to continue expanding his range beyond blockbuster franchises, seeking out independent films and character driven dramas that may challenge him as an actor. He wishes to continue working with directors known for pushing the boundaries of creativity, and eventually may even try his hand at writing or directing his own projects.

Long-term, John is passionate about mentoring young performers, potentially through workshops or a foundation supporting dance and theater education to pay forward the opportunities that shaped his own career at Fancy Feet dance studio. Through these efforts he also hopes to find a worthy successor to Beter Barker and the Arachnoman franchise.
