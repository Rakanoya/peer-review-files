Brief Biography

Carl is a simple man. He lives a simple life. Every day he goes to work, and he brings his a pet cat, Donut. Carl is not allowed to bring the cat to work, but he does it anyway.
Up until 4 years ago, Carl lived a normal life. His childhood was mostly spent in Arizona, where he was born and raised. However, after an incident involving him pushing someone into the grand canyon and stealing their cat, he fled the state.


It would've been smarter for him to flee the country, however Carl is not a particularly intelligent individual. Donut, the stolen cat, is about 107 smarter than him. Since that day, Carl does his best to live an unremarkable life, trying to avoid drawing attention lest his past crimes come back to haunt him.


He is failing, and most experts agree that he will be in prison or dead by the end of the year.


Career Goals

Carl attempts to work as an electrical engineer. He's not very experienced or well qualified, but his uncle is the boss, which affords him many chances to iterate on his mistakes. This is the leverage he hopes to use to become CEO of Business Work Limitedtm


Carl is tragically unaware of his own lack of competence, and therefore his career ambitions are lofty. He hopes to take over for his uncle one day, leading the company into a new era of enlightenment. What he means by this exactly is unclear, but he certainly says it with gusto.
